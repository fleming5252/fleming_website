    Ask our team
    Can it manage more than one branch and a main warehouse?

    Yes. The Super Admin controls the Head Office, main warehouse, branches and users, while each Branch Admin works from a dedicated dashboard for their own branch.

    How do branches request stock?

    A Branch Admin submits a stock request from the branch dashboard. The Head Office reviews it, can approve, modify or reject quantities, dispatches the stock and tracks it until the branch receives it.

    Can delivery personnel sell to customers who are not branches?

    Yes. Route agents can deliver to branches and also sell available stock to other stores, businesses and individual customers along the route, then generate the bill on the mobile app and share it on WhatsApp.

    Does it track batches and expiry dates?

    Yes. Batch numbers, quantities, manufacturing and expiry dates can be recorded, and branches and the Head Office can see batches approaching expiry.

    Can I enter old bills at the price of that date?

    Yes. Pricing is date-based, so backdated transactions use the price applicable on the selected date.

    Do customers need to install an app?

    No. Customers use the WhatsApp chatbot to place order or quotation requests, view account details and purchase history, and receive bills and payment reminders.
