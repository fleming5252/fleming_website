    FAQ

    Questions we hear most

    Something not covered? Our team will walk you through it.

    Ask our team
    Which institutions is it designed for?

    Schools, colleges, coaching centres and training institutes. The platform adapts to different institutional structures, workflows and administrative requirements.

    Which devices and platforms are supported?

    A web application for administration, plus dedicated mobile applications for Android and iOS.

    Can it manage more than one campus?

    Yes. Multi-institution and multi-branch management is supported, with institution-level and branch-level permissions that keep appropriate boundaries between campuses.

    Can parents and students pay fees online?

    Yes. Students and parents can pay through the mobile application, and the system sends payment reminders and confirmations through WhatsApp.

    How many user roles are there?

    Eleven: Super Admin, Institution Admin, Branch Admin, Observer, Academic Head, Class Teacher, Subject Teacher, Reception, Accountant, Student and Parent. Each has its own dashboard and permissions.

    Can we configure it to our own structure?

    Yes. Academic structures, fee structures, user permissions, institutional settings and application branding can all be configured to the way your institution operates.
