FAQ

Questions we hear most

Something not covered? Our team will walk you through it.

Ask our team
Which devices do students use?

Students use dedicated Android and iOS apps. Administrators manage everything through a web-based panel.

Can students learn without internet?

Yes, for supported video lessons. Students can download them for offline viewing.

Can we upload many questions at once?

Yes. Questions can be uploaded in bulk using supported Excel templates, and image-based questions are also supported.

Can we restrict content by plan?

Yes. Subscription plans control access to specific courses and chapters, and to video, audio, live sessions and other features.

How do students sign in?

They register with mobile number verification, then use a PIN or password. Fingerprint and face authentication work on supported devices.

Can we customise the structure and features?

Yes. The learning structure, content, assessments and platform settings can be configured to your institution's requirements.

How do I see it working for my institution?
