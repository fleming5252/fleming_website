FAQ

Questions we hear most

Something not covered? Our team will walk you through it.

Ask our team
Can I upload my entire product catalogue at once?

Yes. Products can be added individually or in bulk with an Excel upload — including names, categories, prices, stock quantities and images.

What can sub-managers access?

Access is role-based. Administrators decide which products, orders, customers and billing features each sub-manager can view or manage.

How do customers receive order updates?

Orders and status updates are sent to customers over WhatsApp, and the integrated order bot handles common requests directly in the chat.

Can I track stock and get low-stock alerts?

Yes. Stock updates automatically as orders are processed, with low-stock and out-of-stock alerts so availability stays accurate.

Does my team need to install any software?

No. The platform runs in a web browser on desktop or mobile — there is nothing to install and updates happen automatically.
