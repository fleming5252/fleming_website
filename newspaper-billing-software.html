Frequently asked questions

Newspaper billing software FAQs.

What is newspaper agency billing software?

It is software that replaces handwritten registers and manual calculations for a newspaper agency. It manages publications, sub-agents, delivery areas, customer bills, payment collections and financial reports in one place.

Can I manage multiple newspapers and magazines in one system?

Yes. You can add different newspapers and magazines, configure their details and pricing, and keep product-wise billing records in the same platform.

Can I create sub-agents and assign delivery areas?

Yes. The administrator can create sub-agents, assign specific delivery areas to each of them and view area-wise distribution records.

What reports does the software provide?

It provides monthly financial reports, area-wise collection reports, agent-wise collection details and total billing and collection summaries.

Who can use this newspaper billing software?

Newspaper agencies, newspaper distributors, magazine distribution businesses, multi-area distribution operations and publication supply businesses.
