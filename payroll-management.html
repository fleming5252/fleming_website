Ask our team
How is the monthly payroll calculated?

Payroll is calculated from each employee's assigned salary structure plus any recorded adjustments. You review the preview, verify the numbers and only then finalise and lock the cycle.

Can I change a salary structure after it is assigned?

Yes. Update allowances, deductions or incentives at any time — every revision is kept in history so past payroll stays traceable.

How do employees receive their payslips?

Each employee gets a detailed PDF payslip they can open and download from the web or mobile app, with a clear breakdown of earnings and deductions.

Does the system support multiple branches?

Yes. Salary assignments and payroll records can be maintained branch-wise, with consolidated reports across the whole organisation.

Who can run payroll and approve changes?

Access is role-based. Administrators, accountants and employees each see only what they need — approvals and corrections stay controlled and recorded.

How do I see it working for my organisation?
