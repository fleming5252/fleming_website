Why it matters

Fruit moves fast. Losses hide faster.

Prices change daily, crates go missing, and credit piles up on notebooks. Three everyday problems the system is built to close.

🚚

Stock leaves, nobody knows what came back

Fruit is loaded onto a route, but sold quantity, leftovers and collections are reconciled by memory.

→ Compare issued, sold, remaining and collected per route.

🧺

Crates vanish quietly

Reusable crates go out with every order. Without a ledger, the balance per customer is a guess.

→ Issued, returned and pending crates, customer by customer.

📒

Credit and prices out of sync

Yesterday’s bill gets entered today at today’s price, and pending amounts live in different books.

→ Date-based pricing and one outstanding balance per customer.

FAQ

Questions we hear most

Something not covered? Our team will walk you through it.

Ask our team
Can I enter yesterday’s bills at yesterday’s prices?

Yes. Prices are stored by date, so backdated transactions use the price applicable on the selected date.

What can salespersons do in the mobile app?

View assigned routes and stores, check stock, create bills with products, quantities and crates, record cash and credit sales, collect payments on pending bills and share bills on WhatsApp.

Can I see where my salespersons are?

Yes. The app supports live location tracking, and movement along routes and visited stores can be monitored from the dashboard.

How are crates tracked?

Crates issued, returned and pending are recorded per customer, including free-issue and paid crates.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. Customers use the WhatsApp chatbot to place order or quotation requests and view account details, purchases and pending credit.

How do I see it working for my business?

Request a product demonstration and we will show the modules that matter for your routes and customers.
