One screen for the whole day

Employees open the app to today’s shift, their assigned site and a single button to check in. Leave, advance and loan requests sit right underneath.

At the site

Location is checked before the punch

Attendance is accepted only when the phone is inside the boundary set for that site. Outside it, the check-in simply isn’t allowed.

Checked in

Stamped with time, site and shift

The check-in is recorded against the employee’s shift and appears straight away in the site and admin views — nothing to retype later.

During the shift

Hours that count themselves

End of shift

FAQ

Questions we hear most

Something not covered here? Our team will walk you through it.

Ask our team
How do employees mark attendance?

Through face recognition or fingerprint authentication on biometric devices, or through the mobile app using GPS location verification. Authorised users can also enter attendance manually.

Can someone edit attendance without it being noticed?

No. Every manual attendance modification is tracked with the details of the user who made it, giving administrators visibility into changes.

What happens when an employee moves to another site?

You transfer them. They don’t need a new registration, and their existing information and records stay with their profile.

Does it handle both salaried staff and daily-wage labour?

Yes. Employees get monthly salary management; labourers get configurable daily or weekly wage structures. Advances, bonuses and other payments are supported for both.

Does it support PF and ESI?

Yes, PF and ESI management is part of employee salary management.

Can I limit what each manager can see or do?

Yes. Role-based access lets you assign people to one or more sites and control permissions for attendance, wages, shifts and reports — for example, one manager can run attendance and wages for a site while another at the same site can only view reports.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. Employees use it for GPS attendance, leave, loan and advance requests. Authorised administrators and managers use it to monitor sites, wages and reports, and to manage transfers.
